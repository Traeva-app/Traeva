# Frankenstein - notes on chapters 1-8

Walton's letters frame the story: he is chasing glory at the North Pole, like Victor.

Victor
- grows up loved, then gets lost in old alchemy books
- builds the creature in secret and runs from it the moment it wakes
- stays silent when Justine is accused

Themes so far: ambition, isolation, who is responsible for the creature.

Questions to talk about
1. Is Victor's silence worse than the creation itself?
2. Why does Shelley open with Walton instead of Victor?
3. Who is the real monster so far?
