Titration lab - data

Goal: find the concentration of the HCl sample.
Setup: 25.0 mL HCl in the flask, 0.100 M NaOH in the burette, 3 drops of phenolphthalein.

Trial 1: 24.7 mL NaOH to the pink endpoint
Trial 2: 24.5 mL
Trial 3: 24.6 mL
Average: 24.6 mL

HCl concentration = (0.100 x 24.6) / 25.0 = 0.0984 M
