# Carbon tax: points for Thursday

- Pollution is an externality: the polluter does not pay the full cost.
- A tax per tonne of CO2 puts that cost back into the price.
- Revenue could be returned to households as a dividend.
- Counterpoint: it hits low-income families harder unless the money is returned.
- Example: a $40 per tonne tax adds about 9 cents to a litre of petrol.

Keep it under three minutes. Leave time for questions.
