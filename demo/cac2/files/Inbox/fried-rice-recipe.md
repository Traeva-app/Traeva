# Vegetable egg fried rice (serves 2)

## Ingredients
- 2 cups of cooked rice, one day old
- 2 eggs
- 1 carrot, diced
- a handful of frozen peas
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Steps
1. Heat the cooking oil in a wok, scramble the eggs, and set them aside.
2. Fry the carrot and peas for 2 minutes.
3. Add the rice, break up the lumps, and fry for 3 minutes.
4. Stir in the soy sauce, the eggs and the spring onions; finish with the sesame oil.

Takes about 15 minutes.
