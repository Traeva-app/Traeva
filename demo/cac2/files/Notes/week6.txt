Review: photosynthesis and respiration

Photosynthesis happens in the chloroplast. Light energy splits water, releases oxygen and makes ATP and NADPH; the Calvin cycle uses them to fix carbon dioxide into glucose.
Respiration happens in the cytoplasm and the mitochondria. Glucose is broken down to carbon dioxide and water, and the energy is stored in ATP.
The two processes are linked: the products of one are the reactants of the other.
